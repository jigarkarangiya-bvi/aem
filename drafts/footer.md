### How to Author the Footer in Google Docs:
1. **Create a 4-Column Table**: Create a standard table with 4 columns. Put the word **Columns** in the top-left header cell.
2. **Columns (Links)**: Type your headings (e.g., "Company") and make them bold. Put your regular links directly below them.
3. **Horizontal Line**: Add a horizontal line (`---`) beneath the table to create a new section for the legal links.
4. **Legal & Socials**: Put your copyright text and links on one line, and your social media icons on another line.
