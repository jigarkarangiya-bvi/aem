# Video Hero Block

This block creates a full-width background video section with text and a button overlaid in the center, and a play/pause button in the bottom left corner.

### How to Author in Google Drive:

1. Create a Table with **1 column** and **2 rows**.
2. In the top merged header row, type: **Video Hero**
3. **Row 1 (Media)**: Insert a link to your video (e.g., an MP4 file). You can also add an image in this cell which will act as the "poster" image before the video loads!
4. **Row 2 (Content)**: Type your Heading, your paragraph text, and insert a Link (AEM will automatically convert the link into the white pill button).

### Features
- **Auto-plays** muted on loop for a seamless background experience.
- The block automatically breaks out of the standard 1200px box layout to bleed full-width to the edges of the screen.
- Automatically adds a subtle dark overlay so white text remains readable regardless of how bright the video is.
- A functional play/pause button is generated automatically in the bottom left corner.
